# Stark Workshop Handbook

## Safety
- The arc reactor must never be serviced alone.
- Fabrication bay doors close automatically after 30 seconds.
- Suit test flights over the Malibu coast require clearance from J.A.R.V.I.S. one hour in advance.

## Coffee
- The workshop coffee machine is called "Dum-E's Revenge". It makes espresso only.
- Smoothies are forbidden near Dummy.

## Contacts
- Pepper Potts approves all purchases above 5,000 USD.
- Happy Hogan drives. Rhodey flies.
