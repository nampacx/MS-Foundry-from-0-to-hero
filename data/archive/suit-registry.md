# Suit Registry

## Mark III
Built in 2008. Status: museum. Power level 61. The first suit with the gold-titanium alloy and stable flight. Its chest plate is held by magnetic locks, not bolts, so it can be removed without tools. The small reactor in the chest is not the workshop arc reactor and has its own manual.

## Mark VII
Built in 2012. Status: destroyed. Power level 78. The first suit with automated deployment: it finds its pilot by two bracelets and assembles in the air. What is left of it is stored as scrap in crate 7.

## Mark XLII
Built in 2013. Status: destroyed. Power level 83. Prehensile modular armor: each piece flies on its own. The pieces lock with quick-release bolts that close by themselves. Nobody tightens them by hand.

## Mark LXXXV
Built in 2023. Status: retired. Power level 97. Nanotech armor with a slot for the Infinity Gauntlet. It has no fasteners at all. It rests in the hangar in a sealed case.

## Storage
The hangar stores 12 suits. Each suit hangs on its own numbered rack and is connected to a trickle charger. A suit leaves the hangar only for a cleared test flight or for the museum.
