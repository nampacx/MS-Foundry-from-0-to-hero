# Arc Reactor Maintenance Manual

## Service intervals
The workshop arc reactor is serviced every 90 days. The last service was on 2026-09-30. A service takes about four hours and needs two people, because the reactor must never be serviced alone. Log every service in the incident log, also when nothing went wrong.

## Housing bolts
The housing is closed with eight titanium bolts. Housing bolts are tightened to 42 Nm in a star pattern, in two passes: first hand-tight, then to the final value with the calibrated wrench from drawer 3. Replace a bolt when its thread shows wear. Never reuse the copper washers.

## Coolant loop
The coolant is a 60/40 mix of deionised water and glycol. The loop holds 18 litres and runs at 2.5 bar. Check the level at the sight glass before every start. Top up only with the premixed coolant from the blue canister, never with tap water.

## Shutdown procedure
Ramp the output down from 3.0 GW to idle over five minutes. Wait until the core temperature is below 40 °C, then close the coolant valves. Hang the red tag on the main switch. J.A.R.V.I.S. confirms the shutdown in the workshop status.

## Spare parts
Spare bolts, washers and one palladium core are kept in the locked cabinet next to the fabrication bay. Tony Stark and Pepper Potts hold the keys. Order replacements as soon as a part is taken, following the procurement policy.
