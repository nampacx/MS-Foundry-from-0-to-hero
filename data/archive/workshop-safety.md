# Workshop Safety Rules

## Working on the reactor
The arc reactor must never be serviced alone. The second person stays at the main switch for the whole time. Wear the grey gloves and the face shield.

## Fabrication bay
Fabrication bay doors close automatically after 30 seconds. Do not block them. The bay is off limits while the printer arm moves. The queue is shown on the status screen.

## Test flights
Suit test flights over the Malibu coast require clearance from J.A.R.V.I.S. one hour in advance. He has to sign off the flight plan before anyone suits up. No flights in fog or after sunset.

## Dummy
Dummy holds the fire extinguisher and uses it often. Smoothies are forbidden near Dummy. Keep cables off the floor in his reach.

## Coffee corner
The coffee machine is called "Dum-E's Revenge". It makes espresso only. Cups stay in the coffee corner, away from the workbenches.
