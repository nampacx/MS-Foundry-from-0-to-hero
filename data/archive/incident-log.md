# Incident Log 2026

## 2026-03-02 · Fire extinguisher discharge
Dummy emptied the fire extinguisher over a toaster that was not on fire. Nobody was hurt. The toaster was replaced, and the extinguisher was refilled the same day.

## 2026-05-19 · Bay door blocked
A crate held the fabrication bay door open for ten minutes. The printer arm stopped by itself. The crate was moved, and the floor in front of the door is now marked yellow.

## 2026-08-14 · Coolant leak
During the morning start, coolant dripped from the reactor housing. Cause: after the last service the housing bolts were torqued to 24 Nm only, which is below the value in the maintenance manual. All eight bolts were retightened to the specified value, and two litres of coolant were topped up. The wrench was sent for calibration.

## 2026-09-30 · Quarterly service

## 2026-10-21 · Unauthorised test flight
