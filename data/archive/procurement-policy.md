# Procurement Policy

## Small orders
Anyone in the workshop can order parts up to 5,000 USD without asking. Use the workshop account and put the order number on the whiteboard.

## Purchase approvals
Pepper Potts approves all purchases above 5,000 USD, up to her limit of 250,000 USD. Anything larger goes to Tony Stark, whose limit is 1,000,000 USD. Send the request with the vendor's quote attached. An order without approval is cancelled.

## Vendors
Order only from vendors in the vendor master. Pym Particle Supply Co. and Rand Corporation are approved vendors. Oscorp Materials and Hammer Industries are blocked: no orders, no payments. A new vendor needs a check by Pepper Potts' office first.

## Invoices
Every invoice needs an order number and a total that matches its line items. Invoices are paid within 30 days. A vendor that sends new bank details by email is called back on the known phone number first.

## Returns
Unused parts go back within 14 days in the original packaging. Palladium cores and coolant cannot be returned.
